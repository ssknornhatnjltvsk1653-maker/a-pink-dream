# A Pink Dream

Create the entire website from scratch as ONE LONG CONTINUOUS SCROLLING EXPERIENCE for my girlfriend after we had a fight.



IMPORTANT: DO NOT make it look like a normal website with separate sections stacked one after another. I want it to feel like ONE HUGE LIVING STORYBOOK / DREAM WORLD that she continuously scrolls through. Every scene should visually connect into the next one. There should be no obvious boxed sections no generic website cards no dashboard layout no grid of features and no boring “section 1 section 2” feeling.



The whole website should feel like she is slowly travelling through one giant cute romantic pink world where the environment keeps changing as she scrolls.



DO NOT USE HER NAME ANYWHERE.



DO NOT use screenshots of our conversations.

DO NOT recreate real messages from our chats.

DO NOT invent specific memories that I have not provided.

DO NOT add fake gifts.

DO NOT make it about breaking up or getting back together.

We are still together. This is simply a beautiful sincere apology and romantic surprise after a fight.



The purpose is:

make her feel loved

make her feel appreciated

make her feel important

show that I genuinely regret letting my ego get in the way

and remind her how much she means to me



The apology must NEVER feel manipulative.

Do not say things like “please don't leave me”

“you are all I have”

“you can't leave”

“I'm nothing without you”

or anything that creates pressure or guilt.



Instead make it warm sincere romantic and emotionally genuine.
